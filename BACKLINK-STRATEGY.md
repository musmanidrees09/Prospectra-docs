## Key Principles

1. **Relevance > DA** — A link from webaim.org (accessibility-focused, DA 70) is worth more than a random directory (DA 90)
2. **Natural anchor text** — Mix "Prospetra", "prospetra.com/free-audit", "accessibility audit tool", and "website accessibility audit"
3. **Content first** — The best backlinks come from content people genuinely want to reference
4. **One at a time** — Don't spam directories. 5 quality listings > 50 low-quality ones
5. **Relationships** — Comment on blogs, share others' content, engage in communities before asking for links
